We are open-sourcing the Valen repository and releasing **Valen-preview-0923** with its corresponding training data.

Valen brings visual perception to System One decision-making: text, images, and video in; decision probabilities out.

<!-- truncate -->

## Open from the start

The repository includes tools for model development, data processing, training, inference, and evaluation. Our aim is to help agents perceive their environment, choose useful actions, and improve through feedback.
